## Where To Find The Rest (In Order of Quality)

### 1. Trainerize Directory — BEST SOURCE (~400,000 coaches)
- **URL:** https://trainerize.me
- Browse by specialty: Online Personal Training, Group Bootcamp, Yoga, CrossFit, etc.
- Each profile has IG link, certifications, and a "Buy Programs" button
- **If they have programs for sale = qualified lead**
- Filter by: Online Personal Training → these are your exact ICP

### 2. Everfit Marketplace
- **URL:** https://everfit.io/marketplace
- Coaches list their coaching services, programs, pricing
- Look for coaches with 1:1 coaching + group programs
- They're already paying $40-80/mo for Everfit = budget for tools

### 3. Stan Store — Search Fitness
- **URL:** https://stan.store (search "fitness", "coach", "trainer", "workout")
- Every coach here is already selling digital products
- They have IG handles in their bios
- Already 7 leads pulled from here

### 4. Gumroad — Fitness Category
- **URL:** https://gumroad.com/discover?query=fitness+coach
- Search: "workout plan", "fitness program", "training guide"
- Sellers with multiple products and reviews = active, qualified
- Already 11 leads pulled from here

### 5. Instagram Hashtag Mining
- Search these hashtags, look for coaches with Kajabi/Stripe/Stan links in bio:
  - `#onlinefitnesscoach`
  - `#onlinetrainer`
  - `#fitnessprogram`
  - `#personaltraineronline`
  - `#onlinecoaching`
  - `#fitnesscoachonline`
  - `#customworkoutplan`
- **Qualifier:** Must have a link-in-bio to a paid product (Stan, Kajabi, Linktree with purchase links)

### 6. Kajabi Creator Directory
- **URL:** Browse Kajabi fitness landing pages via Google: `site:*.mykajabi.com fitness`
- These coaches are paying $149+/mo for Kajabi already

## Qualification Checklist (Must Pass All 3)

- [ ] **Charges money** — Has paid products, programs, or coaching packages visible
- [ ] **Has clients** — At least 10 visible (followers don't count, look for testimonials/reviews/client tags)
- [ ] **Active** — Posted within last 30 days
